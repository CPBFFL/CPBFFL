# CPBFFL weekly editorial standards

- Draft ICYMI, Power Rankings and Predictions for approval before publishing unless explicitly authorized.
- Verify weekly starters, bench, legal positions, scores and H2H. Round player scores down; retain exact team scores and lineup differences.
- Check official pregame injury reports, inactives, practice participation and in-game injury reports for disappointing performances. Distinguish an owner starting an unavailable player from a healthy/active player hurt during the game.
- Be brutal about results, but judge lineup decisions using information available before kickoff. Bench breakouts do not automatically mean bad management. Explain credible reasons for the original start without listing start percentages.
- For counterfactual wins, verify all replacements were legal. Separate one-change wins from multiple-change hindsight combinations. Do not imply a perfect lineup was foreseeable.
- ICYMI: review first, Game of the Week, six pun-titled matchups, exact totals, H2H and records. Pun subjects must be verified starters, or explicitly described as benched.
- Power Rankings: incorporate injury timing, ongoing availability, depth and team performance; avoid treating injury-shortened scores as proof a player/owner failed.
- Predictions: check status and expected workload before a start/sit claim; discuss reasonable alternatives naturally, and conditional outcomes when availability is uncertain.
- Sixth playoff seed always goes to highest season points among teams outside the five other qualifiers. Historical record-based qualification rates are baselines, not personalized probabilities. Use actual winners brackets, not unreliable consolation-inclusive madePlayoffs flags.
